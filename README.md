## Overview

**The Real Windy City** transforms all **804 City of Cheyenne Ch192 public government broadcasts** (`archive.org/details/cochwy`, Seasons 1–15, `2012`–`2026`) and Cheyenne municipal ordinances into an episodic TV broadcast network paired with a **10-Lens Multi-Diet Civic Engine** — backed by automated per-speaker `.mp4` `.zip` archives on GitHub Releases.

Every **Meeting** and every **Ordinance Mini-Series** is rendered from **one unified primary-source database** (`data/civic_tv_db.js` + live `speaker-videos/state.json` feeds from all 6 `cheyenne-archives-*` repositories) and can be experienced through:

### Broadcast TV Framing
- **Procedural WebAudio Intro Theme Song** (3 selectable broadcast scores: *Prime-Time Civic Drama*, *Capitol Ave Synthwave*, *Investigative Legal Noir* + live spectrum visualizer)
- **Animated Opening Montage** (*"Previously On..."* recap -> *Cold Open Verbatim Quote* -> *Opening Cast Credits* -> *Episode Title Card*)
- **Cast of Characters** with speaking-time share, diarized turn count, inline Archive.org clip playback, and direct **1-ZIP-per-speaker** GitHub Release downloads
- **Season Premieres, Season Finales, Three-Act Breakdowns, "To Be Continued...", and "On the Next Episode..."** previews

### 10 Interpretive Lenses (Same Underlying Database)
1. **🎬 TV Episode & Cast** — Serialized recaps, acts, cast cards & cliffhangers
2. **☕ Layman's Narrative** — Plain-English kitchen-table impact & wallet translation
3. **🗞️ Windy City Tabloid** — High-energy Capitol Ave headlines, verbatim zingers, Winners & Hot Seat
4. **⚖️ Legal & Statutory** — Cheyenne Municipal Code, UDC sections, Wyoming Statutes (`Wyo. Stat. § 15-1-103`, `§ 15-1-402`, `§ 15-1-601`, `§ 16-4-401`), standard of review & legal risk
5. **📜 Verbatim & Clips** — Searchable diarized transcript synced to timestamped video playback & speaker `.zip` archives
6. **📊 Civic Analytics** — Speaking-time Gini concentration, debate heat timeline, talk-share bars & turn-transition network
7. **🗺️ Cheyenne Geo-Map** — Interactive vector map of Cheyenne highways, creeks, corridors & geolocated docket pins
8. **🏛️ Historical Vault (2008–2026)** — Cross-era precedent linked into all 6 `therealwindycity/cheyenne-archives-*` repos
9. **💰 Follow the Money** — Fiscal ledger tracking General Fund, 6th-Penny, TIF, Stormwater & contract modifications
10. **🧭 Ordinance Tracker** — Multi-episode legislative pipeline from Planning Commission -> 1st/2nd/3rd Readings

### 4 Viewing & Navigation Modes
- **👈👉 Single Swipe Lens** — Touch-swipe left/right, mouse drag, or `←` / `→` arrow keys
- **✨ Hover Lens Peek** — Hover over any lens button for an instant preview card
- **⚖️ 2-Lens & 3-Lens Side-by-Side** — Independent multi-column comparison synced to the active meeting or ordinance
- **🪟 Floating Iframes Matrix (All 10 Lenses)** — Spawns 10 live `?embed=1&lens=...` iframes in either a **Tiled Bento Grid** or **Free-Floating Draggable Desktop** with one-click **⤢ Expand** full-screen spotlight
